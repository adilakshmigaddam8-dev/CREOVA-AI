CREOVA Sign-In & Access Guide

How Sign-In & Accounts Work

1. Passwordless Email Sign-In

CREOVA uses passwordless email access codes. You never have to remember or manage passwords:

  1. Enter your work or studio email address in the field above.
  2. Click Continue with email. CREOVA will immediately issue a secure 6-digit access code to your inbox.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code and click Verify and sign in to enter your workspace.
  4. Optional passkeys: If your device supports Touch ID, Face ID, or Windows Hello, you can save a passkey for 1-click sign-in on future visits.

2. New Account Registration & Role Selection

If you don't have an account yet, no separate registration link is needed:

  • Simply enter your email address and verify the one-time code sent to your inbox.
  • Upon your first verified sign-in, CREOVA will prompt you to complete your onboarding profile and choose your account role:
    Creator Studio For AI filmmakers, visual artists, and generative creators showcasing portfolios and applying to briefs.
    Brand / Agency For creative directors, brands, and marketing teams publishing briefs, discovering creators, and commissioning work.

3. Verification Code Troubleshooting

If your 6-digit verification code has not arrived:

  • Check Spam/Junk: Occasionally verification emails are routed to promotional or spam folders.
  • Check email spelling: Make sure there are no typos in the email address you entered.
  • Code expiration: Verification codes expire after a short security window. If your code expired or fails, click ← Change email or refresh the page to request a fresh code.

4. Automatic Role-Based Dashboard Navigation

Once authenticated, CREOVA automatically redirects you to your dedicated workspace based on your profile role:

  • Creators reach their Creator Studio (Portfolio Studio, Document Deliverables, Opportunities, Proposals, Engagements, and Verification).
  • Brands and Agencies reach their Campaign Workspace (Brief Management, Creator Discovery, Saved Shortlist, Incoming Proposals, and Deliverables Review).

5. Demonstration Profiles & Public Browsing

Demonstration Fixture Data: The platform database includes seeded fixture profiles for public browsing and preview:

  • Creators: Mira Solanki (demo-creator-01), Theo Laurent (demo-creator-02), Anika Rao (demo-creator-03)
  • Brands: Studio Saffron (demo-brand-01), Still Form Studio (demo-brand-02)

Note: As documented in the project specifications, demonstration profiles are public directory showcase fixtures and do not have live email inboxes. To test live interactive actions (submitting proposals, uploading media, managing engagements), enter your own valid email address to receive real login codes.

6. Administrator Access

Platform administrators sign in through this same secure email-code interface using their authorized account. Server-side role authorization automatically enforces admin privileges for verification evidence reviews. There is no public admin bypass or insecure backdoor.
